Little Dino is an offline dinosaur runner designed for children aged 6–8.

## Information handled by the game

The game does not ask for a name, email address, birthday or account. It does not
include advertising, purchases, analytics, social features, push notifications,
location tracking or online leaderboards. It does not access the microphone,
camera, contacts or advertising identifier. Bundled audio plays locally.

Best scores for each difficulty, collected stars, selected dinosaur color and
game settings are stored locally on the device. The game does not send these
records to the developer or a developer-operated service. Android backup and
device-transfer exclusions are configured for app data.

## Retention and deletion

Local progress remains until the player or parent resets it, app data is cleared,
or the app is uninstalled, subject to the operating system's behavior. Use
**For grown-ups → Reset progress → Erase progress** to reset game records and
settings. There is no server-side account or account-deletion workflow.

## Children

The game is intended for children and does not intentionally collect their
personal information. A parent or guardian can contact the publisher at the
completed contact address above with privacy questions.

## Platform services

Google Play and the device operating system have their own privacy practices,
including store downloads and platform diagnostics. Those practices are covered
by the respective providers' policies. This game has no developer analytics SDK.

## Changes

Update this policy and its effective date when the game changes. Reassess Google
Play Data safety and Families declarations whenever a dependency or feature is
added. This draft describes the current implementation; complete the publisher
contact information and verify the final signed artifact before publication.
