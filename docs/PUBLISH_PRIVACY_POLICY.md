# Publish the Little Dino policy on Notion

1. Copy the complete contents of [PRIVACY_POLICY.md](PRIVACY_POLICY.md) into a
   Notion page, or import the Markdown file. Keep the title, effective date,
   publisher name, contact email and all policy sections.
2. Publish the page as a public, read-only website. Visitors should be able to
   read it without a Notion account, sign-in, invitation or editing permission.
3. Open the public URL in a signed-out/private browser window on a phone and a
   desktop. Verify the full policy and email are readable. A workspace edit link
   or a PDF download is not the public policy URL required by Google Play.
4. Put the same HTTPS URL in the Play Console privacy-policy field and in your
   ignored `android/release.properties` as `privacyPolicyUrl=...`. The release
   helper displays that public URL in the grown-ups area. The full policy is also
   bundled in the app and can be read offline.
5. Keep the page published and update its effective date when data practices
   change. Do not add tracking, forms or requests for children's personal details
   to the policy page.
