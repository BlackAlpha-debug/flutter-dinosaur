# Little Dino Privacy Policy

**Effective date: November 1, 2026**

**Developer and publisher: BlackAlpha-debug**

**Privacy and support contact: [muzammiljawad6@gmail.com](mailto:muzammiljawad6@gmail.com)**

Little Dino is an offline dinosaur-running game designed for children aged 6–8.
This policy explains how information is handled by the Android game and what
happens if a parent or guardian contacts us for help.

## 1. Information the game handles

Little Dino does not ask players to create an account or provide a name, email
address, phone number, date of birth, photograph, or other personal information.

The game saves the following information locally on the player's device:

- Best scores for each difficulty level.
- Collected stars and unlocked dinosaur colors.
- The selected dinosaur color and difficulty level.
- Sound and visual-effects settings.

This information is used to remember progress and preferences. The game does
not send it to us or to a developer-operated server. We cannot view a player's
local progress or restore it remotely.

## 2. Advertising, tracking, and permissions

Little Dino contains no advertising, in-app purchases, accounts, chat, online
leaderboards, social sharing, push notifications, or analytics SDKs. We do not
use the game to track players across apps or websites or to create advertising
profiles. We do not sell or rent personal information.

The game does not access the device's camera, microphone, contacts, location,
or advertising identifier. Game images, fonts, and sound effects are bundled
with the app. Playing the installed Android game does not require an internet
connection.

## 3. Children's privacy

Little Dino is intended for children and does not intentionally collect their
personal information through gameplay. A child can play without providing
personal details or contacting us.

Parents and guardians should send support or privacy requests on a child's
behalf. Please do not include a child's full name, photograph, precise location,
school, or other unnecessary personal details in a message. If you believe a
child has sent us personal information, contact us so we can review the request
and arrange appropriate deletion.

## 4. If you contact us

If you choose to email us, we receive the email address, message, attachments,
and any other information you voluntarily include. We use this information to
answer your question, investigate a problem, or handle your privacy request.
Emailing us is optional and is not required to play.

Support correspondence is handled through Gmail and is processed by the email
providers involved in delivering the message. See the
[Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) for information
about Google's services. We do not use support messages for advertising or
marketing mailing lists.

We keep support correspondence only for as long as reasonably needed to handle
the request, resolve related issues, and meet applicable legal obligations. You
can ask us to delete your correspondence by emailing the address above. We may
need to verify that the request comes from the relevant sender and explain any
information that must be retained to meet a legal obligation.

## 5. Storage, security, retention, and deletion

Local game progress is stored using the device's app-storage and preferences
mechanisms and is subject to the device's normal security protections. Keep
your device and operating system secure; no storage or communication method
can be guaranteed completely secure.

Local progress remains until it is reset or removed through the device's app
data controls or uninstallation, subject to operating-system behavior. To reset
scores, stars, colors, and settings inside the game, open:

**For grown-ups → Reset progress → Erase progress**

This resets progress and restores the default settings. Android cloud backup
is disabled for the app, and backup/device-transfer exclusions are configured
for its data. There is no game account or developer-hosted game database to
delete, and no cross-device progress synchronization.

## 6. Google Play and the public policy page

Google Play, Android, and device manufacturers may process information for
their own services, such as app downloads, updates, security, and platform
diagnostics. Those activities are governed by the relevant providers' policies
and device settings. The game does not include a developer analytics SDK or a
crash-reporting SDK.

The public copy of this policy may be hosted on Notion. Opening that page uses
Notion's website, whose privacy practices are described in the
[Notion Privacy Policy](https://www.notion.com/privacy). Hosting this policy on
Notion does not give us access to a player's locally saved game progress.

## 7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the game or its data-handling practices change.
The updated policy will show a new effective date. The current policy is made
available through the game's grown-ups area and its Google Play listing.
